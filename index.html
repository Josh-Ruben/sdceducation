A group built around possibility.

Shree Devi Education Trust was established in 1992 with a vision of quality education and preparing young people to become capable professionals and upright citizens.

Today, the Shree Devi Group brings together diverse academic pathways across professional education, technology, healthcare, management, design and social work.
